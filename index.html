How I work

At a certain point, what's happening internally doesn't stay internal. It starts shaping how you lead and what you decide. That's where this work begins.

All engagements are built entirely around your specific situation — not a generic version of it.

Reset.

We create the space to surface what is truly going on — beneath the busyness, the roles, and the noise. Before strategy, presence.

Ways to work together

There are three ways to work with Path 9. Each is designed for a different moment — and a different kind of need.

The Single Session

For a specific decision, situation, or crossroads that calls for a trusted outside perspective.

90 Minutes  ·  1:1  ·  Online or In Person

The Single Session. One conversation. Complete in itself.

There are moments — in work, in life, or at the intersection of both — that call for a single conversation. A decision that needs a clear outside perspective. A situation that has become too complex to see from the inside. A conversation you cannot have with anyone currently in your world — because they are too close, too invested, or simply not equipped to hold it. The Single Session is that conversation.

A 30-minute conversation to ensure the Single Session is the right fit.

The Arc

For the work beneath the work. Six sessions through Reset, Reconnect, Realign — two within each phase, allowing depth before moving to what comes next.

6 Sessions  ·  60 Minutes Each  ·  1:1  ·  Online or In Person

The Arc. Reset. Reconnect. Realign.

Deeper work asks for shape and stages. Each phase holds two sessions, giving the work the room it needs before moving to what comes next.

Reset begins where you actually are — not where the role, the responsibilities, or the inherited expectations say you should be. We name the drift, the friction, the quiet erosion that has brought you here. Reconnect returns you to what genuinely matters — your values, your voice, your sense of self beneath the title and the track record. Realign is where it becomes practical — a sustainable path forward, grounded in who you are rather than who you have been performing as.

The Arc holds the work at the depth it asks for.

Path 9 for Organisations

For the weight that sits across a team, a leadership group, or a company. The work is bespoke — for the leader, for the team, or for both.

Bespoke  ·  Individual or Team  ·  Online or In Person

Path 9 for Organisations. For the people who hold the weight of the organisation.

Founders. Directors. The C-suite. The pressure at this level is different — and so is the kind of support it requires.

Path 9 for Organisations is not a packaged corporate offering. It is built around the specific moment your organisation, or the leaders within it, are navigating. Engagements take shape in three ways — bespoke team work, ongoing one-to-one coaching, and in-person residencies — and most clients move fluidly between them.

Main themes:

  • Leadership under pressure
  • Decision fatigue
  • The loneliness of senior leadership
  • Conflict at the top of the organisation
  • Succession, continuity, and legacy
  • Executive transition and reinvention

About

I have spent decades at senior leadership level, working alongside founders, directors, and senior leaders facing complexity, transition, and the quiet friction that so often accompanies success.

I have navigated it from the inside too. There was a point where everything looked right on the outside and felt misaligned on the inside. That experience — of living the drift, sitting with it honestly, and finding a way through — is what Path 9 was built from.

At the heart of the practice is a simple premise: human intelligence is the resource, not the thing being fixed, and learning happens through doing.

Path 9 is built on the Team Mastery methodology — born in Finland's Team Academy. I completed my certified Team Mastery coaching training with Akatemia UK — a programme recognised by the Association for Coaching — and it has been the foundation of my practice since.

Above all, I understand what it costs to carry responsibility over time: the pressure, the ambiguity, the weight of decisions, and the gradual distance that can emerge between outward success and what is felt within. What I bring is a way back.

Sanjeev
