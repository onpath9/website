The Single Session.
One conversation. Complete in itself.

There are moments — in work, in life, or at the intersection of both — that call for a single conversation. A decision that needs a clear outside perspective. A situation that has become too complex to see from the inside. A conversation you cannot have with anyone currently in your world — because they are too close, too invested, or simply not equipped to hold it. The Single Session is that conversation.

A 15-minute conversation to ensure the Single Session is the right fit.

The Arc.
Reset. Reconnect. Realign.

Deeper work requires shape and stages. It benefits from moving through with intention, giving each part of the process the room it actually needs.

The Arc follows the three phases at the centre of Path 9: Reset, Reconnect, Realign. Each phase holds two to three sessions. The arc moves when the previous phase has fulfilled its purpose.

Reset begins where you actually are — not where the role, the responsibilities, or the inherited expectations say you should be. We name the drift, the friction, the quiet erosion that has brought you here. Reconnect returns you to what genuinely matters — your values, your voice, your sense of self beneath the title and the track record. Realign is where it becomes practical — a sustainable path forward, grounded in who you are rather than who you have been performing as.

The Arc holds the work at the depth it asks for.

A 30-minute conversation to ensure The Arc is the right fit.

Path 9 for Organisations.
For the people who hold the weight of the organisation.

Founders. Directors. C-Suites. Leadership teams holding the culture together while everything around them is shifting. The pressure at this level is different — and so is the kind of support it requires.

Path 9 for Organisations is not a packaged corporate offering. It is built around the specific moment your organisation, or the leaders within it, are navigating. Engagements take shape in four ways — bespoke team work, themed mindfulness sessions, ongoing one-to-one coaching, and in-person residencies — and most clients move fluidly between them.

Main themes:

  • Leadership under pressure
  • Navigating uncertainty
  • Preserving culture through change
  • Succession, continuity and legacy
  • Executive transition and reinvention
  • Ethical leadership in practice

Specialist work:

  • The loneliness of senior leadership
  • Mindfulness-led leadership

About

I have spent decades at senior leadership level, working alongside founders, directors, and senior leaders facing complexity, transition, and the quiet friction that so often accompanies success.

I have navigated it from the inside too. There was a point where everything looked right on the outside and felt misaligned on the inside. That experience — of living the drift, sitting with it honestly, and finding a way through — is what Path 9 was built from.

At the heart of the practice is a simple premise: human intelligence is the resource, not the thing being fixed, and learning happens through doing. This is the Team Mastery methodology — born from Finland's Team Academy tradition, recognised by the Association for Coaching, and the foundation of my practice since I trained in it.

Above all, I understand what it costs to carry responsibility over time: the pressure, the ambiguity, the weight of decisions, and the gradual distance that can emerge between outward success and what is felt within. What I bring is the understanding of what it takes to find your way back to yourself within it.

Sanjeev
