Questions

Who is Path 9 for?

Path 9 works with directors, founders, and senior professionals carrying significant responsibility who sense that something in how they are operating needs to change. Our clients are not struggling because they lack capability — they are struggling because something feels misaligned.

Is this the right kind of support for me?

Path 9 is coaching — forward-facing, practical, and built around your professional and personal goals. If you are unsure whether this is the right fit, beginning with a conversation is the right place to find out. Thirty minutes is enough to know.

What kind of commitment does this require?

Real commitment. The work asks you to show up with honesty and to act on what you discover between sessions. The leaders who get the most from this process are those who are genuinely ready — not those who are merely curious.

I have never worked with a coach before. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Many of the leaders Path 9 works with are doing this for the first time. What matters is not prior experience of coaching — it is a genuine readiness to do the work. If something here has resonated, that readiness is probably already present.

What happens when we speak?

It is a conversation, not a pitch. Thirty minutes to understand where you are, what you are navigating, and whether Path 9 is the right fit for this moment. There is no obligation on either side. If it is not the right fit, you will leave the conversation with clarity regardless.
